Saturn Privacy Policy

App by Andrea Poltronieri · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Saturn is an Android app that connects to a compatible smart ring over Bluetooth and keeps health-related readings on your phone. This page describes how Saturn handles information.

What Saturn stores

On your phone, Saturn may store:

  • Readings received from the ring (for example heart rate, oxygen, steps, sleep, and related measures)
  • App settings you choose (language, retention window, alerts, Health Connect options, ring profile)
  • Photos taken with the selfie remote, saved in the phone’s Pictures folder

What Saturn does not do

  • Saturn does not create an account
  • Saturn does not upload your readings or photos to the internet
  • Saturn does not show ads and does not sell your data

Optional features you control

  • Health Connect — if you turn it on, selected readings can be written to Health Connect on the same device, under Android’s permissions
  • CSV export — if you export, the file is created on your phone (or shared with an app you pick, such as Drive or email)
  • Local alerts — if you enable them, notifications stay on the device

Bluetooth and permissions

Saturn uses Bluetooth to talk to the ring, and may ask for related Android permissions (Bluetooth, notifications, camera for the selfie remote, and Health Connect write access when you enable that feature). Those permissions are used only for the features described above.

Data retention

Readings remain on the phone until you delete the app data or until Saturn removes older samples according to the retention period you set in the app.

Children

Saturn is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
